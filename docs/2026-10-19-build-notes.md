## Form 10 — Articles of Amalgamation

New prototype (`/form-10`). Before you begin, then six filing steps.

Before you begin covers the NUANS report (including the 90-day rule and the NUANS site), an Affidavit of Solvency for each amalgamating co-operative, and states there is no filing fee for Articles of Amalgamation.

1. Contact information (cover letter).
2. Business details — primary activity (NAICS) and official email for the amalgamated co-operative.
3. Amalgamation details — add and edit each amalgamating co-operative on its own page (legal name, Ontario corporation number, and date of members’ approval).
4. Co-operative name — **Do you want to change the co-operative name?** Yes asks for a proposed name and NUANS report. No continues without a new name.
5. Supporting documents — one certified amalgamation agreement for the filing. Affidavits of solvency are two per amalgamating co-operative.
6. Review — summary and generated articles PDF.

Draft is saved in session storage.
