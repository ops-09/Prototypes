## Form 3 — Application for Supplementary Letters Patent

New prototype (`/form-3`) under the Corporations Act.

Before you begin explains that a corporation may apply to change its name or to amend objects or other provisions under section 131. The checklist asks for the Ontario corporation number, the approval date, the provisions requested, and supporting documents. Name-change requirements cover the NUANS report, the 90-day limit, separate English and French reports, and consent when a name is similar to an existing one.

1. Contact information (cover letter).
2. Corporation details — Ontario corporation number, legal name, incorporation date, primary activity, and official email.
3. Corporation name — whether the application includes a name change. A new name asks for the name language (English, French, or both) and a NUANS report. This step does not repeat the registry callout or the Ontario corporation number.
4. Supplementary letters patent — resolution confirmation date, the provisions requested, and supporting documents. The insolvency statement sits in the step help, not in the name fields.
5. Review — summary, generated PDF, and authorization.

The home page Form 3 card opens Before you begin.
