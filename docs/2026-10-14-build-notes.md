## Form 1 — Before you begin

`/form-1` is now the instructions page. Title: **Articles of Incorporation of a Co-operative with Share Capital**.

The page lists estimated time, the filing steps, supporting documents (Ontario NUANS report, and Consent to Act as First Director when needed), the $335 standard filing fee, and NUANS and NAICS instructions.

Start continues to the cover letter, which is now Step 1. The home page Form 1 card opens Before you begin.

## Form 1 — directors and incorporators

- A director who is also an incorporator enters share details once. That person is added to the incorporators list. **Add** on the incorporators step creates only incorporators who are not already directors.
- When **Also an incorporator** is No, the director must confirm consent and upload the consent PDF.
- Director and incorporator cards and the review summary show shares and a two-line address.

## Form 1 — filing steps

1. Contact information (cover letter).
2. Co-operative name and NUANS.
3. Co-operative type, primary activity, and official email.
4. Head office address.
5. First directors.
6. Share information.
7. Membership and provisions.
8. Incorporators.
9. Review and certify.
