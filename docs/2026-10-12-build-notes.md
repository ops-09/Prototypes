## Form 1 — Articles of Incorporation (with share capital)

The filing is planned as 8 steps. This build includes the first five. Later steps are not in this build.

1. Contact information (cover letter).
2. Co-operative name, including English, French, or combined name options and a NUANS report.
3. Co-operative type and contact — sub-type, primary activity (NAICS), and official email.
4. Head office address.
5. How many directors? — fixed number or minimum and maximum, then add and edit directors on a separate page. The step shows a linked summary list with remove.

Draft is saved in session storage.

## Form 7 — Articles of Amendment

Five-step filing (`/form-7`).

1. Contact information (cover letter).
2. Business details — registry details, primary activity (NAICS), and official email.
3. Amendment details. Types: change of name, convert to a Business Corporations Act corporation, convert to a Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010 corporation, reduction of authorized capital, and other amendment.
4. Supporting documents — certified resolution and related uploads.
5. Review and validation.
